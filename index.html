Questions

Is this a live answering service?

No. You receive a prerecorded greeting audio file (MP3 + WAV) that plays on your own phone system when callers reach your voicemail or menu. No one answers your calls live, and no calls are routed through us.

Are the voices real people or AI?

They are commercially licensed AI voices, and we say so up front — before you buy, not just here in the FAQ. You choose from four voices (warm female bilingual, warm male, confident professional, and energetic), and your greeting is produced with the voice you pick.

Will it work with my phone system?

Most business phone systems that accept an uploaded greeting file — such as RingCentral and Nextiva — take our MP3 or WAV directly, and your order includes a step-by-step setup guide. Google Voice doesn't accept uploads; for it, we include a play-and-record walkthrough (you play our file while recording your greeting in the Google Voice app). We can't promise every system accepts uploads — if yours turns out not to work with our files at all, that's covered by the refund below.

What does the free revision cover?

Every order includes one free revision for wording, pronunciation, or pacing — for example, changing a sentence, fixing how a name is said, or slowing the greeting down. Just reply to your delivery email and we'll re-record it.

How fast will I get my greeting?

Within 24 hours — and the clock starts when we have received all of your order details (business name, language, voice choice, phone system, and your script or what callers should hear), not the moment you pay. If a detail is missing, we'll email you to ask, and the 24 hours runs from your reply.

What if something is wrong — can I get a refund?

Yes, in defined cases you get a full refund of your $19: if the delivered greeting has wrong words or a mispronounced business name and the free revision doesn't fix it, or if the files can't be made to work on your phone system. Refunds go back to your original payment method.
